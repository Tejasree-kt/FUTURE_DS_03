## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains the Marketing Funnel and Conversion Performance Analysis built during my Data Science & Analytics Internship at Future Interns. The primary objective is to clean raw multi-channel marketing data, evaluate conversion rates across each stage of the user journey (Visitors → Leads → Customers), identify major drop-off friction points, and deliver data-backed recommendations to optimize growth analytics.

* **Total Funnel Impressions:** 1,500,000 — Total audience exposed to marketing campaigns.
* **Lead Conversion Rate (LCR):** 8.2% — Percentage of website visitors converting into registered leads.
* **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** $42.50 — Average budget spent to acquire a single customer.
* **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):** 3.4x — Financial return generated from paid digital channels.

### 📋 Key Findings & Friction Points
* **The Massive Awareness Leakage:** A critical drop-off of **91.8%** occurs between the Visitor and Lead stage, pointing toward landing page speed or complex sign-up forms.
* **High-Intent Drop-offs:** **15.2%** of users who actively add a product to their shopping cart abandon the journey at the final payment page.
* **Channel Imbalance:** Paid Search (Google Ads) and Email Marketing produce the highest conversion rates and ROAS.


## 💡 Actionable Insights & Recommendations
* **Landing Page Optimization:** Simplify the signup flow by implementing one-click Google/LinkedIn login options to reduce drop-offs.
* **Cart Abandonment Dunning:** Automatically trigger a personalized retargeting email within 2 hours of cart abandonment, offering a limited-time 5% discount.
* **Strategic Ad-Budget Reallocation:** Shift 20% of underperforming Social Media ad budgets toward high-converting Paid Search (Google Ads) to maximize conversions.
